A dormant "Send Greetings" feature became a +168% engagement story when we fused Gemini image-gen into it. The unlock was geo-segmentation: 27 festivals across five Indian regions, surfaced at the right moment in the merchant's existing workflow. The AI part did the artwork; the calendar did the work.
</AnswerBox>

Soft SaaS features die by neglect. **Send Greetings in LiveKeeping PRO+** was exactly that: technically present, practically unused. The teams kept shipping heavy, but the light portal was dark.

## Why it sat unused

- The feature existed for years but had no reason to be opened.
- Every greeting-launched list was static; no "when".
- Nothing surprised the user into *trying* the generator.

## The AI reinvention

We integrated **Google Gemini Flash (image), the 'Nano Banana'** so users could build a festival card instantly — no design skills, no assets, no excuses.

But the model alone doesn't fix engagement. The *timing inventory* did:

- A **geo-segmented festival calendar** across five Indian regions
- **27 occasions** — Diwali, Durga, JAYs, Bihu, others — each assigned the right messaging and template
- Triggers placed where users already worked: send-time reminders, invoice completions

## The numbers

| Metric | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feature engagement | dormant | **+168%** |
| Occasions surfaced | ~1 national | **27 geo-segmented** |
| Regions | 1 | **5** |

Users didn't adopt *an AI generator*. They adopted **a card for the occasion before them** — the AI was the paint, the timing was the purchase.

## What I'd do differently

- **Air the calendar earlier.** The 5-region mapping should've shipped before we raised the model; geo-accuracy was the real feature.
- **Test the greeting door.** Our best surface was a banner inside a richer notification — I'd A/B it against "merchant made a bill → invite to wish a client" earlier.

The full build is in the [Send Greetings + Nano Banana case study](/case-studies/livekeeping-send-greetings). It pairs well with the [push notification architecture](/blog/push-notification-architecture) this automated: timing and geo are 90% of the lift.
